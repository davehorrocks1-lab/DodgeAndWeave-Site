Dodge & Weave Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Dodge & Weave is designed to be a simple offline arcade game.

Data collection

The current version of Dodge & Weave does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Device permissions

The current version does not request access to:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos
  • Health data
  • Bluetooth

Analytics and advertising

The current version does not include advertising, tracking technology or third-party analytics SDKs.

Local game data

Game information such as a best score may be stored locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored game information.

Third-party services

The current version does not intentionally send gameplay or personal data to third-party services.

If future versions introduce analytics, advertising, online services or other third-party components, this privacy policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those changes are released.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The current policy will describe the behaviour of the released app.

Contact

A public support/contact method will be added before App Store submission.
